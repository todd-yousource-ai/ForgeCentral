Virtual Trust Zones

A Virtual Trust Zone (VTZ) is a named place in a hierarchy that policies target. You create the zones that describe how your estate is divided, such as departments, sensitive enclaves, quarantine and isolation zones; policies then attach to them (). A zone inherits the restrictions of every zone above it and can only tighten them.

How zones work

  1. A zone's name is its place. A zone's full name is its parent's name, a dot, and its own label: corp.sales.reps sits under corp.sales, which sits under corp. A zone with no parent is a top-level zone.
  2. Zones inherit. A zone takes on every restriction of every zone above it. Its effective posture is its own posture combined with its ancestors', and a restriction anywhere wins.
  3. Tighten only. A zone can add restrictions but never loosen one it inherits; the engine refuses a zone that would. Two domains form a fixed floor that no zone can ever relax: governed egress and execution.

A domain no zone restricts or permits resolves to deny: a zone is fail-closed until policy says otherwise. Zones are resolved each time they are read, so a change is visible at the next read.

Restrictions

  • A zone name is 1 to 16 labels separated by dots, at most 255 bytes in all. Each label is 1 to 63 ASCII letters, digits or hyphens, and starts and ends with a letter or digit. Labels are stored in lower case.
  • Session duration is a whole number of hours from 1 to 24 (default 8). A description is at most 1,024 bytes (text outside ASCII uses more than one byte per character).
  • The editor shows each rule under its field, names what is wrong with a name as you type it, and keeps Create zone and Save changes unavailable until every field meets its rule.
  • A zone that has sub-zones cannot be moved or deleted; its Parent VTZ is disabled.

Moving or renaming a zone does not work in this release: ForgeCentral saves the settings under the new name before it moves the zone. Usually Save changes with a new name or parent fails with The engine refused this change: the zone already exists, no longer exists, or still has sub-zones., and nothing is committed. If the new name belongs to another zone, that zone's settings are overwritten with this form and the move is then refused with The settings were committed; the zone was NOT moved and stays where it was. The same happens if you retype the zone's own name in different capitals. Do not use a new name or parent on Save changes. To restructure, create the zone under its new name, author its policies, then delete the old zone and its policies.

Session duration, telemetry mode, micro-segmentation and lifecycle are recorded with the zone but do not yet change behavior, and a draft zone can receive a distributed policy like a published one. Record your intent in them; do not rely on them.

Deleting a zone does not delete the policies that target it, or withdraw a policy already distributed for it. Delete those policies first ().

ForgeCentral cannot show a zone's stored description. When you edit a zone, the Description field starts empty, and saving replaces the stored description with whatever the field holds. Enter the description again each time you save.

Create a zone

Procedure
  1. Choose Virtual Trust Zones, then New zone. The Configure tab opens a New trust zone form.
  2. Enter the fields (). Under VTZ name, ForgeCentral previews the full name: Commits as corp.sales.reps.
  3. Choose Create zone, then confirm Create corp.sales.reps? with Commit.

The new zone stays open on the Configure tab; on the Active tab its card is highlighted.

Edit a zone

Procedure
  1. On the Active tab, choose the zone's card. The Configure tab opens with the zone selected.
  2. Change the type, session duration, telemetry mode, micro-segmentation or lifecycle. Enter the description again (see the Caution).
  3. Choose Save changes and confirm with Commit.

Delete a zone

Procedure
  1. Delete the zone's sub-zones first (moving them is not available in this release), and delete the policies that target it.
  2. Select the zone, choose Delete zone, and confirm with Delete. The deletion is recorded; the zone's history remains.

Verify

  • The zone's card shows its name, type and, for a draft, the Draft badge. Search zones finds it by any part of its name.
  • Total VTZs counts it. High-sensitivity zones counts zones whose effective posture explicitly denies at least one domain beyond the fixed floor.
  • A zone that recent detections have touched carries a risk badge: Critical when a recent decision recommends escalation, Elevated when one recommends a candidate response, otherwise Nominal. A zone no decision has touched carries no badge.
  • On the Overview, a zone's ring opens it on the Configure tab.
