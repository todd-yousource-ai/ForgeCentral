Overview and the Entity Drawer

The Overview is ForgeCentral's home: a live graph of who and what is connecting to what, grouped through trust zones. You configure nothing on it directly; it is where you notice something and open it. The entity drawer is the detail panel that opens from the Overview, Users, Objects and Logs, and it carries the one action available from anywhere: Isolate.

Read the Overview

  1. Sources: AI agents, users and devices. Select a lane to list its members.
  2. Trust zones: each ring is a zone, colored by its risk band and marked with its posture (Watching, Standard or Quarantine). Select a ring you authored to open it on Virtual Trust Zones>Configure; a demonstration ring opens Configure with no zone selected. When there are more than three zones, use Previous zones and More zones.
  3. Destinations: network, SaaS apps, private apps and data stores. Select one to list the destinations in that category.

Hover over a source or destination to highlight its paths. The header shows the worst risk band across the zones (Risk: Nominal, Elevated or Critical), Live while refreshes succeed, Partial graph when the engine's scan reached its limit, and a banner when a refresh failed and the last graph is still shown.

In this release, the Overview places entities in three built-in demonstration zones (Demo.Users.Public, Demo.Private.Agent, Demo.Public.Agent) by matching parts of entity ids (every other entity goes to Demo.Users.Public), and adds the zones you authored as extra rings that carry no traffic and always read Nominal. The grouping is not operator configuration and does not reflect zone membership you set; zone membership is not available yet.

The entity drawer

The drawer opens from a row on Users>All Users, an object's name on Objects, a member of an Overview list, or a decision on Logs. Close it with its close button, Esc, or a click outside it; when you opened it from an Overview list, the back arrow returns to the list.

Isolate an entity

Isolate from network records, under your identity, that the entity should be moved into a quarantine posture. The record is audited. Because enforcement is off, nothing is blocked and nothing is sent to the entity's endpoint.

Procedure
  1. Open the entity in the drawer.
  2. At the foot of the drawer, choose Isolate from network.
  3. Read the confirmation, which states that live enforcement is off, and choose Isolate. The drawer reads Isolation recorded (quarantine) and audited. Enforcement is off: nothing is sent to an endpoint.

The success message adds that the disposition is distributed to the endpoint. In this release it is not: the disposition is recorded by the Forge engine only. There is no action to lift an isolation, and Isolate from network is offered for objects and network destinations too, where it records a disposition that has no endpoint.
