Logs

The Logs surface is the decision log: every governed decision the Forge engine made from detection, with why it fired. You filter it, follow it live, read a decision's rationale, and create an audited export.

Read the decision log

Each row is one decision: Time (UTC); Decision (the finding and the rule that fired); ATT&CK (the technique); Confidence (the engine's confidence tier); and Outcome, the posture the engine gave it:

  • Escalate: raised for a person to act on.
  • Candidate: promoted as a possible incident.
  • Observe only: recorded and watched, not raised.
Procedure
  1. Choose Logs.
  2. Narrow the list with the filter bar: Search (a finding, rule or evidence), Confidence (High, Medium, Low, Contested; an exact tier, not a minimum), Outcome, and Time range (All time, Last 24h, Last 7 days, Last 30 days).
  3. To stop the list moving while you read, choose Pause; choose Resume to follow it again. The badge reads Live, Paused, or Reconnecting after a missed refresh.
  4. Choose a row's Decision to open Why this decision fired: the finding, rule, ATT&CK technique and tactics, scope and evidence. Choose View beside the acting entity to open it in the entity drawer.

The log shows up to 100 decisions for the current filter, newest first. To reach older decisions, narrow the time range or the search.

Export decisions

An export is an audited engine operation: the Forge engine records a receipt on its audit chain and returns the rows, which your browser saves as decision-log-receipt.json.

Procedure
  1. Set the filters. Choose the Time range again just before you export, so the range ends now.
  2. Choose Export. There is no confirmation step. The page reads Exported N decision(s); audit receipt ... recorded at version V, and the file downloads. A repeated export of the same request reads (already recorded).

An export contains at most the 100 decisions the current filter shows, not every decision that matches. For a larger export, export in several narrower time ranges.

If the export fails, the page reads The export was refused or unavailable. Nothing was recorded.
