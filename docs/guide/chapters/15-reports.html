Reports

The Reports surface has two parts: a twelve-week view of detection volume and coverage, and the incident report, the shaped write-up of one open incident that you can download.

Weekly volume and coverage

The table lists, for each of the last twelve weeks: Firings, Opened, Promoted, Muted, Techniques fired, Incidents opened and Incidents closed. Above it, Corpus coverage now says how many detection rules can be evaluated against the sources this node receives. That figure is the current reading, not a per-week value. If the line adds that incident counts may undercount, the engine's scan reached its limit for the period.

Produce an incident report

Procedure
  1. Choose Reports. Under Incident report, choose the incident from Incident. Only open incidents are listed.
  2. Read the report. A line at the top says where the narrative came from, and each of the six sections is labelled by its source ().
  3. To keep a copy, choose Export as text or Export as JSON.
LabelMeaning
model (adjudicated)Written by the narrative model and checked against the evidence.
engine (record)Taken from the Forge engine's record of the incident.
template (declared fallback)No model output exists for the section, so the declared template is shown. This happens when no narrative model is bound () or no narrative has been generated ().

A report marked needs human review should be read by an analyst before it is shared.

Downloads are made by your browser from the report on screen. They are not recorded in the audit trail. For an audited record of decisions, use the Logs export ().
